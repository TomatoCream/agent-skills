## Persistence rule

- System and home-environment changes MUST go into `~/nix-config`, then rebuild:
  `sudo nixos-rebuild switch --flake ~/nix-config#n1n1 --builders ''`
  (NOPASSWD; pre-approved in `~/nix-config/AGENTS.md`).
- `~/.omp/agent` and `~/.agents` are git-tracked outside NixOS. Never manage or
  overwrite them from nix; commit changes in those repos.
- Anything edited outside those trees is not persistent.
